# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students preparing for the V-ACT competency assessment who need a clear place to explore study topics, practice questions, and build confidence before the exam.

## Product Purpose

Hocluc helps learners prepare for V-ACT through structured subject content, guided practice, and approachable study tools. Success means learners can find a useful next step and understand what to practice.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, encouraging, and clear. The experience should make serious preparation feel approachable while keeping learning goals easy to understand.

## Anti-references

- Repeated promotional sections and content that says the same thing more than once.
- Generic landing page layouts that obscure the learning path.
- Unsupported claims about results, rankings, or learner counts.

## Design Principles

- Show a clear next step for learners.
- Connect each subject with practical ways to learn and practice.
- Make progress feel approachable and achievable.
- Lead with the courses: the home page opens on the catalog by subject, explanations come after it.
- Use photos of Vietnamese students and the calculator app's own screens to support learning, not compete with it; no mascots or generic stock scenes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level is documented. Preserve keyboard access for interactive controls, visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
