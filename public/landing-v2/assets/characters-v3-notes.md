# Character activities v3

Built-in image_gen edits, with CSS motion in index.html.

Prompt direction: Preserve Mai’s identity and palette, change her to a cross-legged seated student writing with a pencil in an open notebook on her lap. Preserve Nam’s glasses, hair and palette, change him to a standing student using a teal calculator and pointing up toward a separate geometry model. Transparent full-body cutouts, crisp 2.5D shading, distinct poses, no waving or book hugging.

Assets: assets/mai-writing-v3.png and assets/nam-discovering-v3.png.

Motion: Mai study tilt and progressive notebook strokes (6 seconds); Nam subtle shift (8 seconds); separate geometry model rotation (9 seconds). These are CSS animations of illustrations and accents, not skeletal hand animation. Reduced-motion disables all loops.

Updated shared character symbols also carry the new poses into course cards. Standalone rebuild embeds both PNG files.
